# 2025 Key Numbers

## Federal

Source: IRS Instructions for Form 1040 (2025), Catalog Number 24811V, Feb 25, 2026

### Filing Thresholds (Single)

- Under 65: $15,750
- 65 or older: $17,750

### Standard Deduction

- Single / MFS: $15,750
- MFJ / QSS: $31,500
- HoH: $23,625
- Additional (65+ or blind): $2,000 single/HoH, $1,600 MFJ/MFS/QSS

### AMT (Form 6251)

- Exemption: $88,100 (single), $137,000 (MFJ), $68,500 (MFS)
- Phaseout starts: $626,350 (single), $1,252,700 (MFJ)
- 28% rate threshold: $239,100 (Form 6251 line 7; $119,550 MFS)
- 28% excess subtraction: $4,782 ($2,391 MFS)
- ShouldFill6251 screening threshold: $232,600 (1040 worksheet line 12; different from actual 28% boundary)

### SALT Deduction (Schedule A)

Source: Instructions for Schedule A (Form 1040) (2025), Catalog Number 53061X, Dec 8, 2025

- Cap: $40,000 ($20,000 MFS)
- Phaseout starts: MAGI > $500,000 ($250,000 MFS)
- Phaseout rate: 30% of MAGI excess over threshold (worksheet line 7)
- Floor (minimum cap after phaseout): $10,000 ($5,000 MFS)
- Worksheet: line 1 = $40,000 cap, line 5 = $500,000 threshold, line 7 = 30%, line 9 = max(line 8, $10,000)

### Social Security Benefits Worksheet (Lines 6a/6b)

- Base amount (line 8): $25,000 single, $32,000 MFJ
- Adjusted amount (line 10): $9,000 single, $12,000 MFJ
- 85% maximum taxable

### Child Tax Credit

- CTC: $2,200 per qualifying child (under 17)
- ACTC (refundable): $1,700
- Credit for other dependents: $500

### Earned Income Credit (Single, no children)

- Max credit: $649
- Earned income ceiling: $19,104 ($26,214 MFJ)
- Investment income limit: $11,950

### New Schedule 1-A Deductions (line 13b)

Available whether you take the standard deduction or itemize.

#### No Tax on Tips (Part II)
- Max deduction: $25,000
- MAGI phaseout: > $150,000 single, > $300,000 MFJ
- Must be in an occupation that customarily received tips before 12/31/2024
- Must not be in a specified service trade or business (SSTB)
- MFS cannot claim

#### No Tax on Overtime (Part III)
- Max deduction: $12,500 ($25,000 MFJ)
- MAGI phaseout: > $150,000 single, > $300,000 MFJ
- Only the FLSA "half" of time-and-a-half qualifies
- MFS cannot claim

#### No Tax on Car Loan Interest (Part IV)
- Max deduction: $10,000
- MAGI phaseout: > $100,000 single, > $200,000 MFJ
- Loan originated after 12/31/2024, new vehicle only, personal use, assembled in US
- Must include VIN on return

#### Enhanced Deduction for Seniors (Part V)
- Max: $6,000 per person ($12,000 if both spouses qualify)
- Born before January 2, 1961
- MAGI phaseout: > $75,000 single, > $150,000 MFJ
- MFS cannot claim

### Other Key Numbers

- SS wage base: $176,100 (max SS tax withheld: $10,918.20)
- IRA contribution limit: $7,000 ($8,000 if 50+)
- Educator expense deduction: $300
- Student loan interest deduction: max $2,500, phaseout at MAGI $85k-$100k single
- QBI deduction (199A) phaseout: taxable income > $197,300 single
- Qualifying relative gross income test: $5,200

### 1040 Line Number Changes vs 2024

- Line 11 -> 11a (AGI)
- Line 12 -> 12e (standard/itemized deduction)
- Line 13 -> 13a (QBI deduction)
- Line 13b: new - Schedule 1-A additional deductions
- Line 7 value -> 7a (capital gain/loss)
- Line 7n: removed
- Line 26 -> 26 value
- Line 28 -> 28 value

### NY Tax Recapture (Single, AGI > $107,650)

Worksheets 7-11 phase in higher effective rates as AGI increases:
- WS 7: AGI $107,650-$157,650, phases in 6% flat (replaces lower brackets)
- WS 8: AGI > $215,400, base 568, incremental 1,831
- WS 9: AGI > $1,077,550, base 2,399, incremental 30,172
- WS 10: AGI > $5,000,000, base 32,571, incremental 32,500
- WS 11: AGI > $25,000,000, flat 10.9%

### NY Itemized Deductions (IT-196)

- No SALT cap (unlike federal $40,000)
- Line 40 overall limit threshold: $340,700 single (3% of excess AGI)
- Line 41 subtraction: state/local income tax + foreign tax only (not property tax)
- Line 46 adjustment (NYAGI-dependent):
  - $100k-$150k: Worksheet 3 phases in 0-25% of line 45
  - $150k-$475k: 25% of line 45
  - $475k-$525k: Worksheet 4 phases in 25-50%
  - $525k-$1M: 50% of line 45
  - >$1M: line 47 = 50% of federal AGI
  - >$10M: line 47 = 25% of federal AGI

### NYC School Tax Credit (Single)

- Line 69 fixed: $63 if income <= $250,000
- Line 69a rate reduction: 0.171% up to $12,000, then $21 + 0.228% of excess
- Eligibility: income <= $500,000 (income = federal AGI minus IRA distributions)
