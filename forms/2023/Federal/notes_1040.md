# 2023 Form 1040 Instructions - Key Numbers

Source: IRS Instructions for Form 1040 (2023), Catalog Number 24811V, Dec 27, 2023

## Filing Thresholds (Single)

- Under 65: $13,850
- 65 or older: $15,700

## Standard Deduction

- Single / MFS: $13,850
- MFJ / QSS: $27,700
- HoH: $20,800
- Additional (65+ or blind): $1,850 single/HoH, $1,500 MFJ/MFS/QSS

## Tax Brackets (Single)

| Rate | Taxable Income Range | Cumulative Tax at Bottom |
|------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| 10%  | $0 - $11,000        | $0                       |
| 12%  | $11,000 - $44,725   | $1,100                   |
| 22%  | $44,725 - $95,375   | $5,147                   |
| 24%  | $95,375 - $182,100  | $16,290                  |
| 32%  | $182,100 - $231,250 | $37,104                  |
| 35%  | $231,250 - $578,125 | $52,832                  |
| 37%  | $578,125+           | $174,238.25              |

Tax Computation Worksheet subtraction amounts (single, for taxable income >= $100k):
- 24%: subtract $6,600.00
- 32%: subtract $21,168.00
- 35%: subtract $28,105.50
- 37%: subtract $39,668.00

## Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet (Line 16)

0% rate ceiling (line 6):
- Single / MFS: $44,625
- MFJ / QSS: $89,250
- HoH: $59,750

15%/20% boundary (line 13):
- Single: $492,300
- MFS: $276,900
- MFJ / QSS: $553,850
- HoH: $523,050

## AMT (Form 6251)

- Exemption: $81,300 (single), $126,500 (MFJ), $63,250 (MFS)
- Phaseout starts: $578,150 (single), $1,156,300 (MFJ)
- 28% rate threshold: $220,700 (all statuses except MFS at $110,350)

## SALT Deduction (Schedule A)

- Cap: $10,000 ($5,000 MFS)

## Social Security Benefits Worksheet (Lines 6a/6b)

- Base amount (line 8): $25,000 single, $32,000 MFJ
- Adjusted amount (line 10): $9,000 single, $12,000 MFJ
- 85% maximum taxable

## Earned Income Credit (Single, no children)

- Max credit: $600
- Earned income ceiling: $17,640 ($24,210 MFJ)
- Investment income limit: $11,000

## Other Key Numbers

- SS wage base: $160,200 (max SS tax withheld: $9,932.40)
- IRA contribution limit: $6,500 ($7,500 if 50+)
- Educator expense deduction: $300
- Student loan interest deduction: max $2,500, phaseout at MAGI $75k-$90k single
- QBI deduction (199A) phaseout: taxable income > $182,100 single
- Qualifying relative gross income test: $4,700
- Additional Medicare Tax threshold: $200,000 single
