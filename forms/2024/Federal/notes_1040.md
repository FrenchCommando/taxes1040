# 2024 Form 1040 Instructions - Key Numbers

Source: IRS Instructions for Form 1040 (2024), Catalog Number 24811V, Dec 16, 2024

## Filing Thresholds (Single)

- Under 65: $14,600
- 65 or older: $16,550

## Standard Deduction

- Single / MFS: $14,600
- MFJ / QSS: $29,200
- HoH: $21,900
- Additional (65+ or blind): $1,950 single/HoH, $1,550 MFJ/MFS/QSS

## Tax Brackets (Single)

| Rate | Taxable Income Range | Cumulative Tax at Bottom |
|------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| 10%  | $0 - $11,600        | $0                       |
| 12%  | $11,600 - $47,150   | $1,160                   |
| 22%  | $47,150 - $100,525  | $5,426                   |
| 24%  | $100,525 - $191,950 | $17,168.50               |
| 32%  | $191,950 - $243,725 | $39,110.50               |
| 35%  | $243,725 - $609,350 | $55,678.50               |
| 37%  | $609,350+           | $183,647.25              |

Tax Computation Worksheet subtraction amounts (single, for taxable income >= $100k):
- 22%: subtract $4,947.00
- 24%: subtract $6,957.50
- 32%: subtract $22,313.50
- 35%: subtract $29,625.25
- 37%: subtract $41,812.25

## Qualified Dividends and Capital Gain Tax Worksheet (Line 16)

0% rate ceiling (line 6):
- Single / MFS: $47,025
- MFJ / QSS: $94,050
- HoH: $63,000

15%/20% boundary (line 13):
- Single: $518,900
- MFS: $291,850
- MFJ / QSS: $583,750
- HoH: $551,350

## AMT (Form 6251)

- Exemption: $85,700 (single), $133,300 (MFJ), $66,650 (MFS)
- Phaseout starts: $609,350 (single), $1,218,700 (MFJ)
- 28% rate threshold: $232,600 (all statuses except MFS at $116,300)

## SALT Deduction (Schedule A)

- Cap: $10,000 ($5,000 MFS)

## Social Security Benefits Worksheet (Lines 6a/6b)

- Base amount (line 8): $25,000 single, $32,000 MFJ
- Adjusted amount (line 10): $9,000 single, $12,000 MFJ
- 85% maximum taxable

## Earned Income Credit (Single, no children)

- Max credit: $632
- Earned income ceiling: $18,591 ($25,511 MFJ)
- Investment income limit: $11,600

## Other Key Numbers

- SS wage base: $168,600 (max SS tax withheld: $10,453.20)
- IRA contribution limit: $7,000 ($8,000 if 50+)
- Educator expense deduction: $300
- Student loan interest deduction: max $2,500, phaseout at MAGI $80k-$95k single
- QBI deduction (199A) phaseout: taxable income > $191,950 single
- Qualifying relative gross income test: $5,050
- Additional Medicare Tax threshold: $200,000 single
